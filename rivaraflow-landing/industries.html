Food waste is your
second-largest cost.

Challenges you're facing

  • Full-buffet waste untracked and unmeasured
  • Large kitchen teams with no waste accountability
  • EU-standard sustainability reporting needed for international guests

What Rivara Flow does

  • Per-station waste logging for buffet and à la carte
  • Real-time alerts when waste exceeds thresholds
  • ESG reports for international tour operators and booking platforms
