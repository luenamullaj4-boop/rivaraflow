Simple by design

How Rivara Flow works.

From kitchen bin to compliance report in five steps. No technical expertise required.

01

Install the smart
bin sensor

Our team installs a compact camera and weight sensor into your existing kitchen bins. Installation takes 2–3 hours and requires no changes to your kitchen layout or workflow. We handle everything — you just carry on cooking.

2–3 hour install No new bins needed Works with any bin size
02

Toss waste
as normal

Staff continue exactly as before — no buttons, no scanning, no extra steps. Every time food is discarded, the sensor automatically records the weight, takes a photo, and classifies the waste category using AI. Zero friction. Zero retraining.

Zero workflow changes Automatic classification Photo evidence logged
03

Data flows to
your dashboard

Every waste event is synced in real time to your Rivara Flow dashboard — accessible from any device, anywhere. You see waste by category, by time of day, by station, and by cost. Finally, the full picture of what's leaving your kitchen.

Real-time sync Any device Cost-mapped data
04

Data generates
your action plan

Rivara Flow's intelligence layer analyses your waste patterns and surfaces specific recommendations: adjust portion sizes on a dish, change procurement quantities for a supplier, or schedule staff training on a particular category. Not generic advice — precise actions tied to your numbers.

Portion recommendations Procurement optimisation Staff training triggers
05

Export compliance
reports

One click generates EU-format waste reports for auditors, investors, and certification bodies. Track CO₂ equivalents, cost savings, and waste reduction progress — all in a format ready for submission. Stay ahead of the regulations before they arrive.

EU-format reports CO₂ tracking One-click export
