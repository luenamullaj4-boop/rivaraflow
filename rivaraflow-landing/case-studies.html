PilotSimulations

A note on our methodology. Rivara Flow is currently in early pilot phase. The case studies below are constructed from industry research, food waste benchmarks from WRAP and the EU Commission, and controlled pilot simulations. We will replace these with real client data as pilots complete. Our commitment to transparency means we will never publish unverified claims.

Pilot simulation scenarios

Pilot Simulation · Restaurant

Mid-size restaurant, central Tirana

60 seats  ·  ~€6,500/month food spend

The Challenge

No visibility into prep waste. The chef team estimated "about 10% waste" — actual measurement revealed 24%.

24% → 11%

waste reduction after 90 days

€845/month

in food cost recovered

100%

EU-ready waste reports from week one

"We thought we had waste under control. The first week of data made it very clear we didn't."

— Restaurant Manager (simulated)
Pilot Simulation · Hotel

Boutique hotel, Sarandë seafront

24 rooms  ·  Buffet breakfast + dinner service

The Challenge

Buffet overproduction at dinner was invisible — staff restocked as a precaution. No data existed to challenge this behaviour.

31%

reduction in buffet overproduction

€1,200/month

recovered from over-catering

ESG report

delivered to international booking partner

"Our international guests started asking about sustainability. Now we can actually answer them."

— General Manager (simulated)
Pilot Simulation · Catering

Events catering company, Durrës

8–15 events/month  ·  100–400 guests/event

The Challenge

Over-catering was the company's safety policy. No client ever complained — but margins were shrinking every quarter.

23%

reduction in per-event food preparation

€3,100/month

recovered across all events

2 new tenders

won citing sustainability credentials

"We started winning tenders we used to lose. Sustainability reporting made the difference."

— Operations Director (simulated)
