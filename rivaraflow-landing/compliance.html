EU Compliance & ESG

Don't wait for the regulation.

EU food waste directives are coming to the Western Balkans. Rivara Flow ensures you're already compliant when they arrive — and that you benefit commercially from being early.

Regulatory Landscape

The regulation
is moving fast.

The EU's Farm to Fork Strategy and the EU Waste Framework Directive are driving mandatory food waste measurement and reporting across all candidate and associated countries — including Albania. The window to prepare is now.

2021

EU Farm to Fork Strategy adopted. Food waste reduction targets set: 50% reduction by 2030 across EU member states.

2023

Albania's EU accession candidacy formalized. Sustainability harmonization begins across Albanian regulation.

2025–2026

CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) begins applying to larger companies in EU. Ripple effects expected in export-facing Albanian businesses.

2027+

Projected mandatory food waste tracking for hospitality businesses in Western Balkans under EU accession framework.

ESG Pressure

Three forces driving
compliance urgency.

Booking platform standards

International OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia) are rolling out sustainability badges. Hotels without verified ESG data will lose ranking visibility to competitors who have it.

Export certification

Albanian food and beverage exports to EU markets increasingly require sustainability documentation. Hospitality businesses in the supply chain need to comply to retain access.

Investor expectations

Growth capital for Albanian hospitality is increasingly tied to ESG readiness. Investors want proof of operational sustainability before committing funds to expansion.

See It In Action

What a report
looks like.

Every Rivara Flow report is structured for regulatory submission and commercial use. Each export includes:

  • Waste by category (prep, plate, spoilage, buffet) with kg and % breakdown
  • CO₂ equivalent calculated per category using EU emission factors
  • Cost impact mapped to € per day and per month
  • Trend analysis vs. previous reporting period
  • Compliance status against applicable regulatory thresholds
  • Exportable in PDF and CSV formats for client or regulator submission
