How it works

How Rivara Flow works

Image placeholder

Step 01

Weigh your waste

Place food waste in the smart bin as you normally would. The Rivara sensor weighs and photographs everything automatically. No buttons. No manual entry. Zero hassle.

Image placeholder

Step 02

Track in real time

Every item is logged instantly to your dashboard. The platform records weight, category, and time so you always know what was wasted, when, and by how much.

Image placeholder

Step 03

Turn waste into savings

Check your dashboard to see exactly what's being wasted. Then take data-driven action — adjust portions, change orders, and start saving money from day one.
