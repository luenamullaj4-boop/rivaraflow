How it works

Three steps.
One closed loop.

No manual logging. No new habits for your staff. The system works silently in the background while you get the data you need to cut costs and comply with EU regulations.

01

Hold food over the sensor

Our camera-and-scale unit mounts inside your existing bin in minutes. No wiring, no construction.

02

Drop it in as usual

The AI snaps a photo and logs the weight automatically — no buttons, no waiting, no extra steps for your team.

03

See what's wasted — and why

Open the dashboard to find cost projections, waste patterns, EU compliance reports, and tailored reduction tips.
