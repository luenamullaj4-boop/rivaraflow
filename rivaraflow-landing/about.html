Our Story

We're turning waste into data.

Rivara Flow is Albania's first smart food-waste management platform — built to help the hospitality industry eliminate invisible losses and stay ahead of EU standards.

Mission

A cleaner kitchen.
A stronger bottom line.

"Food waste isn't a charity issue. It's a profitability issue."

Rivara Flow was built because Balkan hospitality businesses lose 15–30% of their food budget to waste that is never measured, never reported, and never reduced. Every week, kitchens across Albania discard hundreds of euros worth of food — and most managers have no idea it's happening.

Our mission is to change that — not by asking businesses to care about the environment (though they will), but by showing them the money. When waste becomes visible, it becomes manageable. When it becomes manageable, it becomes a competitive advantage.

Rivara Flow gives hospitality operators the data they need to make decisions their competitors can't. That's not altruism — that's good business.

While EU markets have dozens of food waste SaaS tools, the Western Balkans has none built for local conditions. The tools that exist were designed for German supermarket chains and Dutch catering conglomerates — not for a 30-seat restaurant in Tirana or a family agritourism property in the Berat hills.

Rivara Flow is built from the ground up for this gap: the right language, the right pricing, the right integrations, and the right regulatory mapping for a region that is rapidly approaching EU alignment.

The Gap

Why existing tools
don't work here.

Too expensive

Enterprise pricing designed for EU multinationals, not Albanian restaurants and guesthouses. Monthly fees that exceed an entire kitchen's weekly wage bill.

Wrong language, wrong culture

No Albanian-language support. No understanding of the region's hospitality culture, seasonal business patterns, or the informal economy dynamics that shape day-to-day operations.

Requires IT teams

Complex setup that assumes dedicated tech staff and reliable enterprise infrastructure. Most Balkan hospitality businesses have neither — and shouldn't need them.

No local compliance mapping

Built for German or Dutch regulation. Not mapped to Albania's EU accession timeline, the Western Balkans sustainability roadmap, or the specific reporting formats Albanian exporters will need.
