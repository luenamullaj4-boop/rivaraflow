Getting Started

Rivara Flow is a food waste management platform for hospitality businesses. We install a compact sensor on your existing kitchen bins that automatically records every waste event — weight, category, time — and sends that data to a cloud dashboard. You get real-time visibility into what's being wasted, data-driven recommendations to reduce it, and automated compliance reports. No manual entry. No workflow changes.

From signed contract to live dashboard typically takes 5–7 business days. Hardware installation takes 2–3 hours on site. Your dashboard is live the same day. Staff training is built into the onboarding — it takes about 30 minutes and can be done on any device.

Yes. Our Essential plan is designed specifically for independent restaurants and small properties. There's no minimum kitchen size or team size requirement. If you're spending €3,000+ per month on food, Rivara Flow will pay for itself.

No. The dashboard is designed to be used by kitchen managers and owners — not software engineers. If you can use a smartphone, you can use Rivara Flow. Our team handles all technical setup and provides ongoing support.

Hardware

No. The Rivara Flow sensor is a retrofit device that installs into your existing bins. We don't sell bins, and you don't need to replace anything.

The sensor kit includes a camera module, a weight sensor pad, a connectivity hub (Wi-Fi + mobile data backup), and a power adapter. Everything is included in your plan. No additional purchases required.

Yes. The sensor stores data locally if internet connection is unavailable, then syncs automatically when connection is restored. You will not lose waste data due to connectivity issues.

All plans include hardware warranty and remote diagnostics. If a sensor needs replacement, we ship a new unit within 2 business days. Professional and Enterprise plans include same-business-day support response.

The sensor is designed for food service environments. The camera and housing are IP54-rated (splash-resistant), cleanable with standard kitchen sanitisers, and positioned above the bin opening to avoid direct contact with waste.

Platform

The smart sensor captures an image each time waste is deposited. Our computer vision model classifies the waste into categories (vegetables, protein, starch, dairy, etc.) with approximately 85% accuracy. You can review and correct classifications from the dashboard. Each correction improves future accuracy for your kitchen.

Yes. The dashboard is fully responsive and works on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile. There's no app to install. Access from your browser.

Essential plan covers 1–3 bins. Professional covers up to 10. Enterprise is unlimited. Multi-bin setups allow you to track waste by station (prep kitchen, line, buffet, etc.)

Yes. You can create sub-accounts with different permission levels — manager view (full data) and staff view (limited, station-specific).

Compliance

Rivara Flow generates reports aligned with EU Waste Framework Directive requirements, CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) food waste metrics, and GRI 306 (Waste) disclosures. We are actively mapping to Albania's EU accession sustainability framework.

Yes. No personal data about customers or kitchen staff is collected in waste data. Camera images are processed for waste classification and then deleted within 24 hours. Account access data is stored in compliance with EU GDPR requirements.

Yes. The platform maintains a full immutable audit trail with timestamps, bin IDs, and data provenance. Reports can be exported in CSV, PDF, and JSON formats and are accepted by Albanian environmental reporting bodies.
